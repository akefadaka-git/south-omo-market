## 1. Purpose

Define the P3 Seller Workspace requirements building on the accepted P0–P2 customer platform. P3 covers seller access, business profiles, membership, product/service listings, listing moderation, customer inquiry handoff, and seller dashboard capabilities.

This document is the frozen A1 assessment/specification baseline. It does not authorize A2, A3, merge, deployment, or shared database migration.

## 2. Product principles

1. Amharic-first, English-secondary interface.
2. Android-first experience.
3. Initial geographical focus: Koybe and Jinka.
4. One canonical user identity may hold multiple independently authorized roles.
5. User identity, business identity, membership, verification, and permissions are distinct.
6. All access decisions are enforced server-side.
7. No false success messages for operations not confirmed by the server.
8. No fabricated business verification, listing approval, or transaction status.
9. ETB is the initial marketplace currency.

## 3. P3 scope

### P3.1 — Seller access and workspace entry

- Reuse the existing phone-based authentication foundation.
- Detect whether the authenticated user has an eligible seller/business profile.
- Provide seller workspace entry and workspace switching.
- Enforce seller permissions on the backend.
- Treat registration account type as declared intent, not authorization.

### P3.2 — Business onboarding and profile

Support legal business name and public display name; business type and description; primary category; operating town and service area; operating hours; supported languages; business contact details; registration number where applicable; optional geographic coordinates; location visibility preference (hidden, approximate, exact); and explicit consent for location visibility.

Business verification must have an explicit status and auditable history.

### P3.3 — Business membership and access

Roles:
- Owner
- Administrator
- Manager
- Staff
- Finance
- Viewer

Membership lifecycle: `invited → pending → active`, with additional states `suspended`, `rejected`, `revoked`, and `expired`.

Requirements:
- Invitation, acceptance, expiry, suspension, and revocation.
- Business-scoped permissions and audit history.
- Revoking one business role must not disable unrelated roles held by the same user.

### P3.4 — Product and service listings

Sellers can create and save drafts; edit their own listings; select categories and category-specific attributes; provide titles, descriptions, and photos; specify prices or negotiability; use ETB as initial currency; associate listings with the correct business and locality; and submit listings for administrator review.

### P3.5 — Listing publication and moderation

#### First-time listings — approved policy SOM-P3-DEC-002

Every business's first-time listing requires separate administrator approval before publication. Business verification alone does not authorize listing publication. Listings awaiting approval remain `pending_review` and are not publicly visible.

Initial lifecycle: `draft → pending_review → published`.

Other possible states: `rejected`, `paused`, `expired`, `suspended`.

#### Subsequent edits — approved policy SOM-P3-DEC-003

All subsequent edits are subject to moderation rules.

Administrator review is mandatory for changes affecting:
- Price.
- Business identity.
- Business or listing location.
- Safety-related information.
- Material product or service claims.

Minor spelling, formatting, and other non-material corrections may be published directly, provided they generate an audit record.

#### Version and safety controls

- Material edits must not bypass review.
- The last approved listing version should remain available while a material edit is under review, unless a safety or compliance concern requires immediate suspension.
- Rejected edits must not overwrite the last approved version.
- Every moderation decision must be attributable and auditable.
- Suspended businesses must not publish listings.

### P3.6 — Customer inquiry management

**Approved policy SOM-P3-DEC-001: Call/SMS handoff; no in-app chat.**

- Sellers can view inquiries associated with their own listings.
- Sellers may use buyer contact details only where the buyer has permitted disclosure.
- Call and SMS handoff must respect buyer consent and privacy rules.
- Inquiry states: `open`, `read`, `closed`.
- Sellers can filter and manage their inquiry inbox.
- Unauthorized sellers cannot access another business's inquiries.

Explicit exclusions: in-app messaging, chat history, attachments, and real-time chat infrastructure.

### P3.7 — Seller dashboard

Display business profile and verification status; listing totals; pending-review listings; published and active listings; new and open inquiries; and recent business activity. Metrics must be backed by persisted, auditable data. Do not display invented or simulated performance figures.

### P3.8 — Localization, accessibility, and offline resilience

- Amharic-primary and English-secondary interface.
- Android-first navigation.
- Clear loading, empty, error, and offline states.
- Offline draft creation where supported.
- Safe synchronization and conflict handling.
- No confirmation of a server-side action before successful server confirmation.

## 4. Security and governance requirements

1. Authentication identifies the user; it does not grant seller permissions.
2. Business verification and membership authorization are separate checks.
3. All business and listing access is checked server-side.
4. Listing ownership is enforced on every relevant operation.
5. Buyer contact information is protected.
6. Exact location requires explicit consent.
7. Verification, membership, and moderation events are auditable.
8. Unverified or suspended businesses cannot publish listings.
9. Listing review and publication decisions are attributable to authorized administrators.
10. A user's independent roles remain independent when one role is revoked.

## 6. Known assessment gaps and risks

- Seller registration flow requires reconciliation with the general registration and account-role model.
- Global merchant-role assignment based on seller-profile existence requires authorization review.
- Listing submission and publication must consistently enforce business verification, membership, permissions, and moderation policy.
- A complete seller moderation administration workflow has not yet been established.
- Seller replies through call/SMS handoff need privacy and consent validation.
- Dashboard metrics require verification against persisted data.
- Seller navigation integration is not yet confirmed complete.
- Existing roadmap documentation may understate the richer business profile and membership capabilities already present in code. Resolve this discrepancy through controlled reconciliation.

## 7. Explicit exclusions

Outside P3: in-app chat; digital payments and wallet transactions; delivery coordination; marketplace commissions and subscriptions; paid advertising; advanced analytics; P4 maps expansion; unapproved admin-platform expansion; production deployment; and shared database migration.

These require separate scope and authorization.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

When P3 implementation is separately authorized, it must demonstrate:

1. Authenticated sellers enter only authorized business workspaces.
2. Business profiles support the specified information and verification lifecycle.
3. Membership roles and permissions are business-scoped.
4. Unauthorized users cannot modify another business's listings.
5. First-time listings cannot become public without administrator approval.
6. Material edits require administrator review.
7. Minor edits can publish directly with an audit record.
8. Rejected edits do not overwrite approved versions.
9. Suspended businesses cannot publish listings.
10. Sellers access inquiries only for their own listings.
11. Call/SMS handoff respects buyer contact permissions.
12. Dashboard metrics reflect persisted and auditable data.
13. Amharic and English experiences are usable.
14. Offline behavior does not falsely report uncommitted server operations.
15. Automated tests cover authorization, moderation, ownership, privacy, and listing lifecycle.

## 9. Approved policy decision register

- **SOM-P3-DEC-001 — Inquiry response model:** Approved. Call/SMS handoff, without in-app chat.
- **SOM-P3-DEC-002 — First-time listing publication:** Approved. Separate administrator approval for every business's first-time listing submission.
- **SOM-P3-DEC-003 — Subsequent edit moderation:** Approved. Administrator review for material changes affecting price, identity, location, safety, or material claims; minor non-material corrections may publish directly with audit records.
